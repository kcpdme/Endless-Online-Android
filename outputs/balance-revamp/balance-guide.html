A reason to play every class.
A worthwhile upgrade after Oron.

This implementation concentrates the main equipment journey into levels 1–50. Existing level and EXP records stay intact. Levels above 50 remain possible. The original cubic EXP curve and the world's existing EXP rate are preserved.

Class roles and attributes

ClassBuild and role
PriestWIS first, then CON and INT. Holy Light for solo play; strongest party healing.
MagicianINT first, then CON and WIS. Burst damage and area spells spend TP.
RogueAGI first, then CON and STR. Accurate melee, evasion and Twin Strike.
ArcherAGI first, then CON and some STR. Equip a bow and arrows; use range and Aimed Shot.

Warrior has the strongest HP/armour foundation. Rogue trades some durability for AGI accuracy and evasion. Archer gains distance through native bow attacks. Magician has strong TP-funded burst and bounded area damage. Priest has affordable Holy Light for solo play and the strongest healing. Summoner uses CHA/INT attacks and one real spirit companion. Only Priests cast healing spells. Potions remain common recovery for every class.

Class identity and safe class changes

The server checks current class and level when equipping, training and casting, and again when a spell executes. Changing class returns incompatible weapons, arrows and clothes to your bag and recalculates their bonuses. Learned off-class skills remain owned, visibly locked until you return to their class. At the inventory hard cap a worn copy stays inert rather than being deleted. Bows and arrows belong to Archers; daggers and Hidden Blade specialise in Rogue; staves, vestments and spirit foci have their own class owners. Starter tools, ordinary accessories, costumes and selected hybrid armour remain common.

What Summoner does

Summon Spirit Wolf at level 8 (18 TP; 2 TP every 2 seconds), Spirit Wurm at 25 (30 TP; upkeep 3), and Spirit Drake at 40 (45 TP; upkeep 4). One visible native companion follows you for up to 90 seconds. It assists the monster you actively attack and stops five seconds after your last attack. Its damage scales with CHA, INT and level. Recasting replaces it; Dismiss Companion is free. Mana exhaustion, changing class, travel, death or logout removes it. Spirits are PvE assistants: they cannot tank, drop loot, fight in PK areas or farm unattended. Their damage uses the owner’s normal kill/reward attribution. The pet is part of Summoner’s combined damage budget.

Equipment variety and crafting

Heavy weapons trade accuracy for a harder hit; Rogue precision weapons add accuracy and evade; spears improve reach-themed attack stats; caster foci add TP. Warrior armour adds HP/CON, Rogue clothes evade, Archer clothes accuracy, and caster robes mana and their primary attribute. Recipe-only equipment gets a bounded crafting edge. The new level-25 Rogue Hidden Blade and level-8 Reinforced Hunting Bow are distinct crafted definitions; buying and crafting the same item ID produces the same stats. Cosmetic recolours remain equivalent.

Progression at 1, 8, 15, 25, 35 and 45

Oron / Orona sits at level 25. Hero Plate and Draco provide level-35 alternatives. Baru at 45 improves every Oron combat bonus; its recipe consumes Oron and six Baru Flocks. Every class also gets a level-45 weapon and male/female body reward. Intermediate equipment is sold by existing shops; final recipes use earlier gear, Dragon Claws, Dragon Wings and Gold.

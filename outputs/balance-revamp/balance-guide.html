Running

Walking is normal speed for every class. Enable Run for ×1.25 speed, or ×1.5 for Rogues. It drains 1.5% maximum TP per second while enabled, including standing still, and stops automatically at zero TP. Fractional costs accumulate. Only Magicians use area spells; all other class skills, including Priest heals, select one target. Warrior attribute defence is doubled, and its existing doubled shield armour remains.

Original EXP progression

Monster rewards use the original supplied ENF EXP values at ExpRate 100% (×1). Quest GiveEXP actions grant their written values directly. No global quest multiplier is applied. Existing character EXP and levels are preserved.

Classic newborn and Peasant foundation

A bare level-zero character starts with 10 HP, 10 TP, damage 0–1, zero accuracy, evasion and defence, and no free attributes, equipment or spells. Unassigned characters and Peasants use classic attribute-earned stats and the original hit/damage calculations for their outgoing and incoming combat. The original Peasant outfit adds one evasion and one defence. The two-Goat-Horn Dagger keeps its original damage 1–2 and accuracy +1. Other professions retain their modern class abilities and combat formulas.

Selected progression encounters from recommended level 8 gain 15% HP and approximately 10% attack damage over profile 13, rounded to whole native stats. Attack speed, accuracy, evasion, armour, EXP and drops do not increase. Crow, Rat and Blob return to their original combat fields; all Tutorial Island NPCs retain original combat fields. This separates classic onboarding from later class progression.

Class identity and safe class changes

The server checks current class and level when equipping, training and casting, and again when a spell executes. Changing class returns incompatible weapons, arrows and clothes to your bag and recalculates their bonuses. Learned off-class skills remain owned, visibly locked until you return to their class. At the inventory hard cap a worn copy stays inert rather than being deleted. Bows and arrows belong to Archers; daggers and Hidden Blade specialise in Rogue; staves, vestments and spirit foci have their own class owners. Starter tools, ordinary accessories, costumes and selected hybrid armour remain common.

What Summoner does

Choose one of 16 pets from the Aeven Eggkeeper (35,46, beside the market) or hunt its monster for a bound egg. Normal pets start at level 5, Rare at 10, Epic at 20, Legend at 30 and Mythic at 40. Each tier shares damage, accuracy, hit limit, attack interval and TP costs; appearance never grants hidden bonuses. Higher tiers improve damage and hit reliability with higher TP upkeep. Hatching adds a permanent pet and a bound Hatched Egg keepsake. Bind one pet through the top egg icon. Dismissal, TP exhaustion and all deaths start a shared five-minute recovery, during which no pet can be rebound or summoned. Travel and logout preserve the collection without starting recovery. Pets assist owner attacks and retaliate when an aggressive monster hurts the owner. Monsters never target pets; upkeep continues while called. Pets give no separate rewards.

Equipment variety and crafting

Heavy weapons trade accuracy for a harder hit; Rogue precision weapons add accuracy and evade; spears improve reach-themed attack stats; caster foci add TP. Warrior armour adds HP/CON, Rogue clothes evade, Archer clothes accuracy, and caster robes mana and their primary attribute. Recipe-only equipment gets a bounded crafting edge. The new level-25 Rogue Hidden Blade and level-8 Reinforced Hunting Bow are distinct crafted definitions; buying and crafting the same item ID produces the same stats. Cosmetic recolours remain equivalent.

Progression at 1, 8, 15, 25, 35 and 45

Oron / Orona sits at level 25. Hero Plate and Draco provide level-35 alternatives. Baru at 45 improves every Oron combat bonus; its recipe consumes Oron and six Baru Flocks. Every class also gets a level-45 weapon and male/female body reward. Intermediate equipment is sold by existing shops; final recipes use earlier gear, Dragon Claws, Dragon Wings and Gold.

Carrying capacity

Total carried weight counts the full quantity of bag items and every equipped item. Only weight strictly above capacity blocks walking and player trading; equality is allowed. Admins use real weight and normal capacity too. Trade invitations, acceptance and final net weight are checked natively on both sides. A rejected trade resets both confirmations without moving items. You can remove offers, cancel, drop items or deposit into a nearby locker to recover. Gold and existing weightless potions keep their defined zero weight.

Eggs cannot be traded, dropped or shared in chests. A hatched egg permanently unlocks its pet and becomes a weightless Hatched Egg keepsake. Hatched Eggs cannot be sold, traded, dropped or discarded; duplicates remain unhatched for sale. Pets survive PK, arena and ordinary deaths. All pets share a five-minute recovery after dismissal, TP exhaustion or death. Use the top egg icon to choose the bound pet after recovery. Travel and logout do not start recovery.

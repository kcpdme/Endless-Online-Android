# Playable painted character pilot

Scope: two coordinated, composed peasant outfits (male/female), Brown Boots
graphic 4, default skin 0 / hair style 1 / hair color 0, unarmed and no hat or
shield. Maps and NPC graphics remain shared. Other equipment combinations use
the complete Classic appearance. Modular painted equipment follows this pilot.

1. Bake the selected painted direction artwork with a cutout mesh rig into
   idle, four walking phases, two melee phases, cast, reach, chair and floor
   sitting poses for all four directions. Keep native size and feet anchors.
2. Publish the two complete appearances into a new immutable pack release.
   Keep Classic copies, source sheets and previous releases intact.
3. Enable Enhanced and add animated male/female comparison previews to Graphics.
   Show current-character coverage clearly; preserve exact appearance matching.
4. Verify all directions/poses, unsupported-outfit fallback, original Wraith
   behavior, switching and restart persistence, and pixel-identical rollback.
5. Build, verify, install and exercise the enhanced pack on Android. Record
   screenshots and package/texture-size measurements. Commit and push the result.

Animation is a deliberately limited first cutout rig, rather than individually
painted frames. The pilot establishes movement/readability and reversible asset
delivery before expanding the wardrobe, weapons and remaining graphics.

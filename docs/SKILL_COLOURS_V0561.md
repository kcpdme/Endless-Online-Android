# Skill colours and pet controls, Android 0.56.1

Skill colours follow the effect instead of just the profession. Fire uses red/orange with a yellow core, Thunder yellow, healing green, ice cyan, energy turquoise, holy attacks gold, spirit and arcane attacks violet, arrows green/teal, and physical strikes copper/red/steel. Bard notes are pink. Green Flame remains green and Dark Bite uses crimson/violet. Two-colour shading, tinted backgrounds and existing rune details keep small icons readable. The icons remain procedural vectors; no raster artwork pack is added.

Summon and dismiss commands are omitted from both learned skill-book entries and trainer offers. They cannot be assigned to quick-skill slots, including through stale selections. Existing pet shortcut assignments clear and save through the normal preference mechanism; new defaults skip pets. Native pet ownership records remain intact, and the dedicated pet button still calls the bound pet or dismisses the active pet. Summoner offensive abilities remain in the skill book.

Run moves to the left of the bottom Chat button and follows the composer when the keyboard opens. It retains its 68 px target and native TP/speed behaviour. Footer buttons take precedence over the joystick activation area. On the narrowest landscape layout the stick is reduced slightly and raised to leave the footer clear. The top egg collection control sits between Admin and Zoom; common header buttons use tighter spacing to fit. The egg remains Summoner-only.

Validation: 1,119 rendered HUD, skill, colour and egg checks at 960×540, 1280×720 and 1544×720; 483 classic-dialog/HUD checks; 75 chat/zoom checks; 16 shortcut persistence checks; existing movement-control regression. Tests cover old pet slot cleanup, filtered trainer entries, preserved ownership, summon/dismiss requests and Run touches inside the joystick activation region. Godot import/runtime checks and the signed Android export also pass. Server combat rules and native pet storage are unchanged.
